CargoSphere: one shared platform for every party in a shipment

CargoSphere is an ecosystem for end-to-end logistics and supply chain operations. It isn't just a document tool. Everyone involved in a shipment comes to the same platform, does their own part of the job and shares documents with the other parties, all on one shared shipment record.

The problem: A single shipment passes through many companies and teams. Each handoff ran on paperwork, emails, spreadsheets and phone calls. Documents were re-typed, lost or chased, and nobody had one view of where the shipment stood.

How the platform solves it: It digitises those process flows, so the manual work and paperwork move into one place.

  1. 1 · Own roleEach party signs in and sees only the tasks that are theirs.
  2. 2 · Do the workThey complete their step and upload the documents it needs.
  3. 3 · Share onceEvery other party sees the update in the same shared record.

What I do: As APM / Business Analyst, I study how each party works today, find where the manual effort and paperwork pile up, and turn that into digital workflows. So far that's 15+ workflows and 40+ features, each with a PRD, user stories, acceptance criteria and edge cases. One example: supporting documents had to work across many document types and roles, so I defined one configurable document workflow instead of a separate flow for each.

verifiedWork that used to happen on paper and over email now happens in one place, with less manual effort and one source of truth for every party.
