Product management · Logistics

A configurable document workflow for CargoSphere

Problem

Supporting documents had to move through many document types and business roles, and building a separate flow for each one would not scale.

What I did

I owned the feature from discovery to UAT. I chose one configurable workflow over one-off flows, wrote the PRD and user stories, and covered business rules, validations and edge cases before development started.

Outcome

Document handling became consistent and scalable across the product.
